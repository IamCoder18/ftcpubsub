# FTC Integration

These are the types you touch in every OpMode. They are the glue between the FTC SDK lifecycle and the orchestrator.

- **[SafeOpMode](/docs/ftc/safe-opmode)** — the base class. Creates the orchestrator, gives you five lifecycle hooks, and enforces the hardware-thread rule on every `loop()`.
- **[HardwareActions](/docs/ftc/hardware-actions)** — the facade over the hardware thread: `run`, `call`, `callAsync`, `bulkRead`.
- **[SafeDevice](/docs/ftc/safe-device)** — a typed wrapper that routes one device's calls through the hardware thread. Also covers `SafeHardwareMap`.
- **[GamepadAdaptor](/docs/ftc/gamepad-adaptor)** — publishes every gamepad button and axis onto topics at 60 Hz.
